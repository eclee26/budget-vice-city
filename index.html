On foot, move in the direction of the camera with WASD or the arrow keys. Drag the view to change direction. Stop the car before getting out. Hold the brake at a standstill to select reverse; hold the accelerator at a standstill to return to forward gear.

On foot, use the left thumbstick to move; push farther to run. In a car, move it left or right to steer, then use GAS and BRAKE separately. Drag the open view with another finger to look around.
