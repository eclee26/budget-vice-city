# GTA V-inspired quality scorecard

This is an internal, provisional visual/gameplay assessment against a conceptual reference. These numbers are judgments informed by the listed observations; they are not a measured percentage of GTA V or an independent user study.

| Category | Weight | Provisional score | Evidence / remaining gap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Open-world quality | 20% | 5.0 | Four districts, nine office/apartment/warehouse styles, taller towers, coast, reserved gardens/parking and collision-preserving rendering cells. Procedural repetition remains visible. |
| Vehicle physics | 20% | 6.0 | Fixed-step spring/tyre/contact model, 18 model checks, browser driving and terrain-support checks. No full 3D rollover/airborne rigid body. |
| Graphics and lighting | 20% | 4.5 | PBR surfaces, procedural textures, reflections, day/night, shadows, fog and inspected captures. Asset complexity and surface artwork remain below the reference. |
| Character movement | 10% | 3.5 | Walk/run/jump and entry/exit work. Keyboard movement and animation still need a dedicated improvement pass. |
| NPC and traffic AI | 10% | 5.0 | Curved lane paths, signal awareness, following/junction tests, fleeing and police road routing. Pedestrian behavior is simple. |
| World interactivity | 10% | 5.0 | Jobs, police, services, parking and persistent career state. Interiors and a broader mission set remain absent. |
| Browser performance | 10% | 5.5 | Resource counts stay bounded; GTX 1650 High samples exceed 60 FPS at 720p. A repeatable preset-switch render warm-up stall and broader hardware coverage remain open. |

Provisional weighted average: **5.0/10**. Target: weighted 6.0/10, with every category at least 5.0. **The target is not yet verified or achieved by this scorecard.** Performance results cannot compensate for missing character/art quality. See `GAME_PLAN.md` for the next priorities and `GAME_TEST_RESULTS.md` for objective results.
