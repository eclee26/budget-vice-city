# GTA V-inspired quality scorecard

This is an internal, provisional visual/gameplay assessment against a conceptual reference. These numbers are judgments informed by the listed observations; they are not a measured percentage of GTA V or an independent user study.

| Category | Weight | Provisional score | Evidence / remaining gap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Open-world quality | 20% | 5.0 | Four districts, nine office/apartment/warehouse styles, taller towers, coast, reserved gardens/parking and collision-preserving rendering cells. Residents now concentrate locally and refill offscreen while traveling. Procedural repetition remains visible. |
| Vehicle physics | 20% | 6.0 | Fixed-step spring/tyre/contact model, 18 model checks, browser driving and terrain-support checks. No full 3D rollover/airborne rigid body. |
| Graphics and lighting | 20% | 4.5 | PBR surfaces, procedural textures, reflections, day/night, shadows, fog and inspected captures. Profiled characters, fitted garment details and body/hair/outfit variety improve crowd presentation. A user-supplied detailed Civic improves one car exterior/cockpit; other asset complexity, facial performance and surface artwork remain below the reference. |
| Character movement | 10% | 4.5 | Camera-relative keyboard/touch motion, normalized diagonals, acceleration/braking, capsule footprint with wall sliding, rotated-car clearance/separation, buffered single-press jumping, distance-driven gait and landing pose. Twelve controller checks and desktop/mobile browser input checks pass. Rig/art, climbing, ragdolls and animation variety remain basic. |
| NPC and traffic AI | 10% | 5.5 | Curved traffic lanes, signals, following/junction checks and police road routing. Pedestrians use local personal space, all-car approach prediction, actual-body impacts, route-preserving escape, conversations, idle gestures, horn reactions and clear-space recovery. Body-aware spacing/contact checks and actual-world pause/pooling/traffic tests pass; natural idle/talk states occur in a sustained sample. Dialogue/schedules, crossings and ragdolls remain gaps. |
| World interactivity | 10% | 5.5 | Named passenger jobs include visible boarding/exiting and seated riders, seat belts, outfit variation, checked paths, comfort/time fares and saved progress. Rider geometry fits seven models, with render interpolation and transfer checks. Last Light now connects two named contacts, on-foot carrying/loading, graded driving and a handoff with saved progress. Authored story depth, interiors and deeper character interactions remain limited. Original skin/glass now hinge at real front apertures, with restored closed geometry and seven-model checks; inner trim, Van art and transfer animation remain basic. |
| Browser performance | 10% | 6.0 | Stable preset/day-night lighting configurations avoid repeated shader variants. Registered styles prepare during loading in the actual composer output format: the reproduced 1.28 s cold downtown frame falls to ~105 ms, with 56 programs retained and zero compile/link calls during the diagnostic. Preparation costs ~1.14 s before readiness. Streaming lifetime and actual desktop/touch-layout RAF tests pass. Ordinary 100 ms cold frames, initial loading latency and real phone/modest-GPU coverage remain gaps. |

Provisional weighted average: **5.3/10** (5.25 before rounding). User target, updated 2026-10-21: **8.0/10 relative to GTA V**. **The target is not achieved.** Movement, rendering stalls, ambient reactions and one mission loop have improved; inspected captures still show basic procedural art, repetitive streets and a simple human rig. The prior failed cold-scene diagnostic is retained as history and now passes after explicit preparation during loading; this does not certify flawless frame pacing or total startup latency. Tests establish specific behavior, not an objective GTA V quality percentage. Performance results cannot compensate for missing mission/character/art quality. See `GAME_PLAN.md` for next priorities and `GAME_TEST_RESULTS.md` for objective results.


2026-10-22 review: Last Light and the supplied Civic add concrete interaction and vehicle detail. Overall remains **5.3/10**; one short chapter and one imported car do not support a broad quality increase or an 8/10 claim.
